# Vision & Goals

## Vision

Build an extensible AI platform that can provide realistic, context-aware interactions for different users, roles, and organizations.

## Goals

### Product Goals

- Make AI interaction useful and contextual.
- Preserve relevant conversation history.
- Support multiple user and organization contexts.
- Keep the product extensible beyond the MVP.
- Provide a foundation for future personalization and analytics.

### Engineering Goals

- Keep modules loosely coupled.
- Separate domain logic from infrastructure concerns.
- Use clear API boundaries.
- Design authentication and authorization for future role expansion.
- Avoid hard-coding organization-specific behavior.
- Keep web and mobile clients compatible with the same backend contracts.

## Non-Goals for the MVP

The MVP should not attempt to implement every future organization, role, AI capability, or analytics feature.

Future functionality should be introduced through well-defined modules rather than tightly coupling it to the initial implementation.
